    Amateur Arena Duel

    Enter from opposite gates, maneuver through five phases per turn, and destroy the rival vehicle. This first playable build automates movement timing, handling, control checks, directional armor, basic machine-gun fire, and arena-wall collisions.

    Prototype scope: simplified bends, drifts, targeting, internal damage, and AI. It is not yet the complete classic ruleset.
